Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide warranty on printer parts?▼
Yes! All new original parts come with a 7-day replacement guarantee. Refurbished parts carry a 3-day check guarantee. If the part is defective on arrival, we replace it free of charge.
How long does delivery take?▼
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours (Mon–Sat). Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days for most locations in India. Metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore) usually receive orders in 2–3 days.
Are GST invoices provided?▼
Yes, we provide proper GST tax invoices for all orders. All listed prices are exclusive of 18% GST. The invoice will be included in your shipment and also emailed to you.
Do you offer bulk/wholesale pricing?▼
Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders (10+ units) and for service centers/resellers. Please WhatsApp us with your requirements for a custom quote.
How do I return a wrong or defective item?▼
Contact us within 7 days of delivery via WhatsApp with your order number and photos of the defective product. We will arrange a free pickup and replacement within 2–3 business days.
What payment methods do you accept?▼
We accept all online payment methods — UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets via Razorpay. We also offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for most pin codes across India.
How can I track my order?▼
After your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number. You can track your order on our Track Order page or directly on the courier website. You can also WhatsApp us with your order number for instant updates.
